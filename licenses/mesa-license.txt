Mesa 3D Graphics Library — license and copyright

Clicker's Windows installer includes Mesa binaries — opengl32.dll,
libgallium_wgl.dll and dxil.dll — in the mesa\ directory beside the executable.
They are unmodified, they are loaded only on a machine whose own OpenGL cannot
draw, and they may be replaced with any compatible build. The driver they are
here for is llvmpipe, which rasterises on the processor.

Two things follow. First, Mesa's own license page, verbatim, as published at
https://docs.mesa3d.org/license.html. Second, the MIT license it names, which
is the license the core library and the drivers shipped here are under; Mesa
keeps the full set of texts its individual files may use in the licenses/
directory of its source tree, at https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa.

llvmpipe links LLVM, whose license is beside this file in llvm-license.txt.

Mesa implements various APIs, including OpenGL, OpenGL ES, Vulkan and OpenCL.
Even though Mesa implements these APIs, the implementation isn't formally
conformant on all combinations of drivers and hardware. Khronos maintains lists
of conformant implementations for each of their APIs, as well as trademark
details.
